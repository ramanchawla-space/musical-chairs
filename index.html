How to play
  1. The host shares the room link. Everyone picks a name and a man or a woman to play as.
  2. When the music plays, everyone dances round the chairs automatically. Watch the pattern!
  3. When the music stops, run to a free chair: WASD/arrows, or click / tap where you want to go.
  4. There's always one chair too few. No chair? Booted for the rest of the level, but you keep your points.
  5. Five levels, each harder than the last, and everyone's back in for each one. Thrones, silver chairs and gold/silver tiles pay extra. Most points wins.
