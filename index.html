How to play
  1. The host shares the room link. Everyone picks a name and a picture.
  2. When the music plays, everyone dances round the chairs automatically. Watch the pattern!
  3. When the music stops, run to a free chair: WASD/arrows, or click / tap where you want to go.
  4. There's always one chair too few. No chair? Booted. Last one sitting wins.
